# Technical debt register

Intentional shortcuts, with the milestone by which each should be resolved.

| ID | Where | Debt | Why accepted | Resolve by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TD-1 | `interaction/SceneCache`, `Operation::setValue` | Tessellation and preview booleans run synchronously on the GUI thread. After the 2026-09-25 bounding-box fix, a push/pull preview on a 21-face filleted part costs 43 ms, almost all of it the boolean (38 ms where the pushed face meets tangent fillets); tessellation is ~1 ms. Fine for typical maker parts; still visible while dragging near fillets and on heavy parts. Fix: run previews on a worker thread, latest value wins, keep the last good mesh on screen. | Simplicity while the interaction model settles. | M3 |
| TD-2 | `selection/Picking.cpp` | Brute-force ray/triangle and edge-segment picking, O(triangles) per hover. | Correct and simple; models are small. | M3 (BVH) |
| TD-3 | `geometry/TopoSignature` | Signature-based topological naming only; can mis-resolve on symmetric parts after large upstream edits. | Awareness now, provenance naming when history editing ships. | M4 |
| TD-4 | `geometry/Tessellation.cpp` | `BRepMesh_IncrementalMesh` stores triangulation inside the shared TShape, so tessellating is a hidden mutation of an "immutable" `Shape`; not safe to tessellate one shape from two threads. | Single-threaded today. | Before TD-1 |
| TD-5 | `render/` | QRhi via `Qt6::GuiPrivate` ties binaries to the Qt minor version. | Only way to use QRhi; widely done. | Review per Qt upgrade |

| TD-17 | packaging (**blocks distribution**) | MSYS2's OpenCASCADE links its visualization toolkit, which pulls in an FFmpeg built with `--enable-gpl` plus x264/x265 (GPL) and, through FreeImage/libtiff, jbigkit (GPL-2.0); THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt in the package lists every bundled package's license. `dist/OpenShape` therefore contains GPL-licensed DLLs OpenShape never uses. | TD-18 | `ui/AppController` | One `stateChanged` signal drives ~25 QML properties. | ~~TD-21~~ | `geometry/Modeling.cpp` `pushPullFace` | **Mostly resolved** (`pushPullFaceKeepingEdges`, new steps): fillets and chamfers around a pushed face move with it when everything the moving band crosses is straight walls; otherwise: push/pull is prism + boolean + unify. Fillets and chamfers next to the face do not follow it (a step appears instead), and tangent contact makes the boolean slow. Shapr3D moves the face and re-intersects its neighbours. A per-face move now exists (`offsetFace`, used for holes and curved faces) but refuses faces whose neighbours are tangent fillets. | Robust and simple for boxy parts. | M3: move a face together with its tangent fillets (re-fillet, or a `LocOpe`-style tweak), then let push/pull use it with prism + boolean as the fallback. |
| ~~TD-22~~ | `document/` | **Resolved**: "Split into bodies" (the body's actions, its flagged Model-panel row or step; a step that leaves pieces says so in a message) adds a `Split` step that keeps the largest piece and makes every other piece a body whose base `SplitPiece` step takes it from the parent's shape before the split, matched by solid signature (volume, centroid, box). Left: the pieces are matched by nearest signature, so after a drastic upstream edit two similar pieces can swap bodies; a piece split off a body cannot be subtracted from that body (dependency cycle; Union and Intersect work: the piece keeps the result; or delete the split step); the body a piece was split off cannot be deleted while the piece exists: Delete hides it instead (no "make independent" that would freeze the piece's shape). | | |
| TD-23 | `interaction/AlignOperation`, `MoveFeature` | Align is a one-time placement stored as a motion; it does not follow its target when that body changes later. Mirror planes and pattern axes picked from faces/edges are stored as geometry too. | Predictable, simple files; Shapr3D aligns once as well. | If the owner wants linked placement: store target references and re-resolve on recompute. |
| ~~TD-24~~ | `interaction/RotateOperation` | **Resolved**: a clicked straight edge or hole/shaft becomes the axis (one ring), a clicked corner or circle moves the pivot, "Center pivot" goes back; stored as a Move step with its center and axis.
