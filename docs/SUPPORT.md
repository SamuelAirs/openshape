# OpenShape support

Help for OpenShape on iPhone, iPad and Windows.

## How do I …?

- **The user guide**, step by step with pictures:
  [docs/USER_GUIDE.md](USER_GUIDE.md). In the app, the **?** button opens a
  card with every tool and gesture.
- **Where are my projects?** On iPhone and iPad in the Files app: *On My
  iPhone* (or *On My iPad*) → *OpenShape*; exports (STL, 3MF, STEP) in its
  *Exports* folder, from where you can send them to a slicer, AirDrop or
  iCloud Drive. On Windows, wherever you saved them.
- **The app closed while I was working.** Start it again: it offers to
  restore your unsaved work.

## Report a problem or ask a question

- **GitHub issues:** https://github.com/SamuelAirs/openshape/issues (free
  GitHub account needed; what you post is public). Please say which device
  and version (File → About OpenShape) you use, what you did, what you
  expected and what happened; a screenshot helps.
- **Testing a beta in TestFlight?** Take a screenshot in OpenShape and send
  it with a note from TestFlight.
- **Email:** **[OWNER: add a support email address here before submitting to the App Store]**

The log can help with a crash or something slow: on iPhone and iPad it is
in the Files app (*OpenShape → Logs → openshape.log*), on Windows in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenShape\OpenShape\logs\openshape.log`. It lists the names
and folders of files you opened, so look through it before you post it.
